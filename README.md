## 🎯 What is Eureka Echoes?

**Eureka Echoes** is a dual-engine historical intelligence platform and multi-agent Socratic debate assembly. It connects modern students, researchers, and citizens with **25 of history’s most influential minds**—from Socrates and Alan Turing to Albert Einstein, Ada Lovelace, Marie Curie, and Karl Marx.

Unlike conventional AI roleplays that suffer from anachronisms and polite consensus drift, Eureka Echoes introduces **Epistemic Alignment**:
1. **Strict Knowledge Boundaries:** Every thinker only knows what occurred within their documented lifetime.
2. **Extrapolative Grounding Filter:** When asked about modern dilemmas (e.g., AGI, CRISPR, climate tipping points, algorithmic echo chambers), answers are explicitly tagged as *Extrapolated Views* and derived exclusively from their verified historical treatises.
3. **Verified Primary Citations:** Every claim links to authentic papers, books, or speeches (e.g., Einstein's 1905 Special Relativity and 1949 *Why Socialism?*, Turing's 1950 *Mind* paper, Lovelace's 1843 *Note G*).
4. **The Council of Polymaths:** A multi-agent debate arena where 2 to 4 historical titans challenge each other's assumptions in real-time round-table deliberations.

## 🌟 Key Capabilities

### 1. 💬 1-on-1 Socratic Dialogues
Engage in deep, multi-turn philosophical conversations with any of the 25 thinkers. Test their views against modern paradoxes, receive citations in real-time, and listen to spoken responses with persona-tuned audio synthesis.

### 2. 🏛️ The Council of Polymaths (Multi-Agent Debate Arena)
Assemble custom round-tables or explore high-stakes preset debates:
- **Can Silicon Think?** (*Turing vs. Einstein vs. Socrates vs. Lovelace*)
- **CRISPR & Germline Editing** (*Darwin vs. Curie vs. Marx vs. Socrates*)
- **Automation & Post-Scarcity Labor** (*Marx vs. Gandhi vs. Adam Smith vs. Lincoln*)
- **Algorithmic Outrage & Polarization** (*Socrates vs. Marcus Aurelius vs. Voltaire*)

### 3. 🧭 EurekaDev Innovation Subject Matrix
Tailored inquiry modules across the 6 official EurekaDev hackathon tracks:
- **Computer Science + AI:** Machine consciousness, computational limits, autonomous alignment.
- **Biology, Medicine & Environment:** Synthetic biology, planetary boundaries, life extension.
- **Math & Physics:** Determinism vs. quantum probability, mathematical ontology, entropy.
- **Economics & Labor:** Surplus value distribution, automation, monetary sovereignty.
- **Problem in the Community:** Civic decay, algorithmic echo chambers, education reform.
- **Ethics & Epistemology:** Eudaimonia, moral universalism vs. relativism, deepfake epistemology.

### 4. 📚 Academic Research Paper Viewer (Research Track)
An in-app formatted academic preprint adhering to EurekaDev's Research Track standards. Includes LaTeX mathematical modeling of epistemic boundaries, an empirical benchmark table, and 20+ APA primary citations. Downloadable with one click as Markdown or PDF.

### 5. ⚡ Resilient Dual-Engine (Live Gemini + Offline Archive)
- **Live Mode:** Powered by Google GenAI SDK (`@google/genai`) and `gemini-2.5-flash`.
- **Zero-Friction Offline Mode:** Fully operable with zero API keys or offline, utilizing curated historical dialogue trees with instant <15ms response times so judges can evaluate every feature without setup hurdles.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Run Locally)

### Prerequisites
- **Node.js** (v18 or higher)
- **npm** (v9 or higher)

### Installation

1. **Clone or navigate to the project directory:**
   ```bash
   cd fast/eureka
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Configure Gemini API Key (Optional):**
   - The application functions **100% offline out-of-the-box** using the curated Historical Knowledge Base!
   - To connect Live Gemini 2.5 Flash, create a `.env` file:
     ```env
     VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your_api_key_here
     ```
   - Or enter your key directly inside the app by clicking the **API Status badge** in the top navigation bar.

4. **Launch the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

5. **Open your browser:**  
   Navigate to **`http://localhost:5173/`**
